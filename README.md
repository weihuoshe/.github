# 围火社 · Hearthring Studio

**围一团火，造千万个世界。**

Hi! We are **围火社**, a small independent game studio started by two university roommates from **Meizhou, Guangdong, China**. We met at **Jiaying University** and share the desire to build unusual, thoughtful games.

We love systems that create unexpected stories, playful experimentation, and memorable worlds.

> Two friends, one small fire, countless worlds.

### What we're making

We're exploring original indie games — particularly strategy, tower defense, and roguelike ideas. More to share as our projects develop.

### Follow our journey

This is our official GitHub home for public tools, prototypes, and repositories that we're ready to share. Some game development work may remain private during production.
